# Heart Rate Monitor Workout

## Overview
The "Heart Rate Monitor Workout" feature allows you to perform a workout using **only** a Heart Rate Monitor (HRM) as the primary device. This is ideal for activities where you don't have a smart fitness machine (like a smart bike or treadmill) but still want to track your effort and categorize your activity.

## Supported Devices
*   **External Bluetooth Heart Rate Monitors:** Chest straps, optical armbands, etc.
*   **Internal Heart Rate Sensors:** Built-in sensors on Android Watches (Full Android Watch - FAW).

## How to use

1.  **Open the Menu:** From the main screen or recording screen, open the menu (FAB).
2.  **Pair HRM:** Select the **Heart Rate Monitor** pairing option (Heart icon).
    *   *Note: If you have no other devices connected, the app might prompt you to connect one. Select the Heart Rate Monitor options.*
3.  **Choose your Sensor:**
    *   **External:** Wait for the scan to find your Bluetooth device and tap to connect.
    *   **Internal (Watch):** Tap "Use Watch's Sensor".
4.  **Select Sport:** A dialog will appear asking you to **Select Sport**. Choose the activity you are about to perform (e.g., Cycling, Running, Rowing, Yoga, etc.).
    *   *Why is this important?* Selecting the correct sport ensures your workout is saved correctly and that other sensors (like the Internal Cadence Sensor) calculate the right metrics (RPM vs SPM).
5.  **Start Workout:** 
    *   **External HRM:** The workout will usually start automatically after connection.
    *   **Internal HRM:** After pairing, close the menu and **tap the connected Heart Rate Monitor icon** on the main screen to start the workout.

## Internal Cadence Sensor Integration
If you are doing a rhythmic activity (Run, Cycle, Row) using an HRM, you can also enable the **Internal Cadence Sensor** to track RPM/SPM using the device's accelerometer.
*   See [Internal Cadence Sensor](internal_cadence_sensor.md) for more details.
